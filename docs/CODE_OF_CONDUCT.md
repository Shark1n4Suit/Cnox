# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

The SAIC project is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community focused on advancing secure AI deployment technology.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* **Professional Communication**: Using clear, respectful language in all interactions
* **Constructive Feedback**: Providing thoughtful, actionable feedback on code and documentation
* **Security Mindfulness**: Responsibly discussing and reporting security-related topics
* **Inclusive Collaboration**: Welcoming newcomers and helping them contribute effectively
* **Knowledge Sharing**: Sharing expertise while being patient with different skill levels
* **Innovation Focus**: Maintaining focus on advancing AI deployment security and performance
* **Respect for Diversity**: Acknowledging and respecting different perspectives and experiences
* **Community First**: Prioritizing the health and growth of the SAIC ecosystem

### Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered harassment and are unacceptable:

* **Discrimination**: Any form of discriminatory language or behavior
* **Harassment**: Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption
* **Security Violations**: Malicious disclosure of vulnerabilities or security information
* **Spam and Off-topic Content**: Excessive promotional content or irrelevant discussions
* **Doxxing**: Publishing private information without explicit permission
* **Professional Misconduct**: Misrepresenting credentials, plagiarism, or academic dishonesty
* **Disruptive Behavior**: Deliberately derailing discussions or creating hostile environments
* **Legal Violations**: Any illegal activities or encouraging others to break the law

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the SAIC project in public spaces. Examples include:

* **Project Repositories**: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and code reviews
* **Communication Channels**: Project Discord, Slack, mailing lists, or forums
* **Events**: Conferences, meetups, workshops, or presentations about SAIC
* **Social Media**: When representing the project on Twitter, LinkedIn, or other platforms
* **Professional Settings**: When discussing SAIC in professional or academic contexts

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please report it to the project team at:

**📧 GitHub Issues**: [Report via GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Shark1n4Suit/Saic/issues) with "conduct" label  
**🔒 Security Issues**: [Report security issues](https://github.com/Shark1n4Suit/Saic/issues) with "security" label (or email if sensitive)

All reports will be handled with discretion. In your report please include:

* Your contact information
* Names (real, nicknames, or pseudonyms) of any individuals involved
* When and where the incident occurred
* A detailed account of what happened
* Any additional context or supporting materials

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction
**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning
**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved for a specified period of time. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban
**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban
**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment, or aggression toward individuals or classes of individuals.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the project community.

## Professional Standards

As SAIC targets enterprise and production environments, we maintain additional professional standards:

* **Security Responsibility**: Report security issues through proper channels
* **Quality Standards**: Contributions should meet professional development standards
* **Documentation**: Clear, accurate documentation is essential for enterprise adoption
* **Backward Compatibility**: Consider the impact of changes on existing deployments
* **Performance Awareness**: Be mindful of the performance implications of contributions

## Recognition

We recognize that building innovative AI deployment technology requires diverse perspectives, expertise, and collaboration. Contributors who exemplify these values help make SAIC a leader in secure AI deployment.
